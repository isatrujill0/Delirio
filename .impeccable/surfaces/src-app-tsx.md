# NEXO device screen (src/App.tsx)

Scope: the entire on-device app for an 11" iPad Air in landscape. Mode: Operate. Code-led build.
seed: none (pinned) — the user's reference image pins the world.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The screen IS the bedside device, not a web page. A fixed instrument panel: left rail, live stage, and a persistent hardware row (− ▶︎ + 🔔) drawn as controls rather than as a picture of hardware. Refuses the scrolling page and the dashboard grid.

OWN-WORLD: Deep indigo night (#14164a→#2a2c78) lit from below by a lavender horizon glow. Translucent lilac panels with a 1px light rim, real offset soft shadows, and rolling dune waves in violet at the foot of every screen. Lexend for legibility; line icons at 1.75 stroke; the active item is a filled lavender pill.

STORY: In one glance: hello, the day, the time, where I am, and that my family left me something. Each section answers one question: who am I with (Mi gente), how do I calm down (Mi mundo), what is happening to me (Mi cuidado), where am I (Mi guía).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Inicio. Greeting and a large live clock on the left, the family message card with its photo as the hero on the right, three context tiles below (place, care, weather), and the wave horizon.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Pressing play on a family message: the photo card lifts, the waveform breathes, and the voice reads the message aloud.

MOTION GRAMMAR: Slow (400–700ms) exponential ease-out; screens cross-fade with a 12px rise; the waves drift at 40s cycles; everything is static under reduced motion.
